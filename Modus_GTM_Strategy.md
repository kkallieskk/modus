## STRATEGIC NORTH STAR

Modus is not a discovery platform. Discovery is a solved (if imperfect) problem. Modus is the **operating layer** — the contract, the escrow, the compliance, and the payout rail — that makes every influencer marketing transaction trustworthy enough to happen at scale with creators who have never had a bank-verified invoice in their lives.

**The one-line pitch:** *"Run legally compliant influencer campaigns with verified regional creators — without a CA, an agency, or a WhatsApp nightmare."*

## PART 3: CREATOR ACQUISITION STRATEGY

### 3.1 — The Single-Player Utility: Free Tool Before Any Campaign Exists

**Name:** *"Modus Rate Card"* — a.k.a. the **Creator Income Estimator**

**What it does:**
A creator visits modus.in/rate-card, inputs:
1. Platform (Instagram / YouTube / Moj / ShareChat)
2. Follower count
3. Average views per video/Reel
4. Content category (food/fashion/agri/lifestyle/comedy)
5. City/State

**Output:** A beautiful, shareable PDF "Creator Media Kit" showing:
- Their benchmark rate per post (with min/max range)
- How they compare to similar creators nationally
- What a legally compliant creator invoice should look like
- What brands can legally deduct as TDS from their payment
- A QR code to join Modus and receive campaigns

**Why this works as single-player utility:**
- Every creator *wants* to know what they should charge
- 85% have never had a media kit — this gives them one instantly
- They share it on Instagram stories (free distribution)
- It solves a *real, immediate problem* with zero dependency on the two-sided marketplace being active
- It's a trojan horse: creators who generate a media kit are pre-qualified leads who are actively thinking about monetization

**Distribution:**
- Post the tool link in every creator community/group
- YouTube Shorts: "Jaano apna sahi rate" (Know your right rate) — demo video
- WhatsApp forward-able message: Hindi text + link
- Creator Scouts share it at meetups via QR code

**Conversion funnel:** Rate Card → Profile creation → Marketplace listing → First campaign application

### 4.2 — Sales Playbook

#### Sales Motion: Consultative + POC-first
*No demos without context. No pitches without a specific pain point identified first.*

**Stage 1: Pre-call Research (10 minutes)**
Before any outreach, document:
- Brand's last 3 influencer posts (look for missing "Paid Partnership" disclosures)
- Whether their invoice publicly mentions TDS
- Their product category + target customer geography
- Whether they have regional language content at all

**Stage 2: First Touch (LinkedIn DM or Email)**
Reference *their specific situation*. Example:
> "Noticed your collaboration with @creator_xyz last week — no ASCI disclosure tag was used, which technically creates liability. Also: did you deduct 194R TDS on that payment? Happy to explain what it means for your accounts. Zero obligation. 15 minutes?"

**Stage 3: Discovery Call (30 min)**

*Questions to ask (in this order):*
1. "Tell me about your last influencer campaign. What worked, what didn't?"
2. "How did you find those creators? WhatsApp groups? An agency?"
3. "How long did it take from briefing to live content?"
4. "Did you deduct TDS on creator payments?"
5. "Has your CA flagged any compliance risk on marketing spend?"
6. "What percentage of your customers are outside the top 6 metros?"
7. "Have you ever tried working with creators from UP, MP, Rajasthan, or regional vernacular creators?"

*Listen for:* Frustration with delays, compliance anxiety, desire to reach Tier 2/3 customers, previous bad agency experience. These are the buying signals.

**Stage 4: Demo (30 min, personalized)**

Never show a generic demo. Before the call, pre-build a mock campaign in Modus using:
- Their actual product category
- A creator from a state/language relevant to their target customer
- A sample brief that mirrors what they'd actually send

Show: Campaign Builder → Creator Search with regional filter → Escrow flow → Compliance check → TDS documentation generation → Creator Insights dashboard

*Anchor statement during demo:* "What you're looking at is the entire workflow your agency does, at 1/3 the cost, with a full audit trail your CA can access directly."

**Stage 5: POC Offer (Close)**

*"Let's run one campaign together before you commit to anything. ₹0 platform fee. You just pay the creator. We handle everything — brief, compliance, escrow, TDS docs. If you don't see value, you don't pay us ever. Deal?"*

## APPENDIX A: THE MODUS COMPETITIVE MOAT STACK

| Moat Layer | Description | Time to Build |
|-----------|-------------|---------------|
| **Network Moat** | Every brand-creator transaction strengthens both sides. More brands → more campaigns → more creators monetizing → more creators joining → more brands can access regional supply | 6–12 months |
| **Creator Trust Moat** | KYC-verified, rate-card educated, escrow-protected community in 10 languages. Supply competitors cannot replicate without 2+ years of relationship building | 9–18 months |
| **Compliance Moat** | TDS certificate library, ASCI audit history, 7-year financial records = brands cannot easily switch without losing their compliance paper trail | 12–24 months |
| **Data Moat** | Proprietary regional creator performance database (CPE by state, language, category — nobody else has this) | 18–36 months |
